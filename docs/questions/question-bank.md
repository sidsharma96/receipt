# Question bank

These questions test whether you can explain Receipt without the book open. They come in three levels:

- [Newcomer](newcomer.md): what Receipt does, and the basic concepts and primitives it uses.
- [Practitioner](practitioner.md): how each part works here, and why it was chosen over the obvious alternative.
- [Expert](expert.md): failure modes, traps, costs, the threat model, and what you would change.

Each answer is hidden until you open it. Try to answer in full sentences first, then compare. An answer ends with links to the section that teaches the topic and, where it helps, to the exact lines of code. Trap questions also describe the plausible wrong answer and why it is wrong for this code.

The [working guide](../WORKING-DOC.md) ends each chapter with selected questions
from this bank. Edit questions here; the chapter copies are generated from them.
